Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how Sterling Method ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and with Sterling Method Uploader, an internal tool we use to manage our own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services.

Who uses the tool

Sterling Method Uploader is a private, internal application. It is used only by the owner of the Sterling Method YouTube channel to manage that single channel. It is not offered to the public and no other user can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

Our tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through the YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • Our own channel data: channel name and ID, our own videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status and publishing schedule, so we can upload and schedule our own videos.
  • Public comments on our own videos: the text of comments and the public display name of the commenter, so we can read viewer feedback and occasionally mention a comment in a future episode.

We do not access, collect or store data from any other channel, and we do not access private data of viewers.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish and manage content on the Sterling Method channel and to read feedback from our audience. OAuth tokens and working files are stored locally on the channel owner's computer. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Comment data used to prepare an episode is kept for no longer than 30 days and then deleted. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Cookies and this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Revoking access

Access granted to Sterling Method Uploader can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Viewers who want a comment removed

If you commented on one of our videos and want us to stop using your comment in any future episode, email us and we will remove it from our records.
